# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, trolling, threats, publishing another person's private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct issues through a private channel offered by the repository. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
